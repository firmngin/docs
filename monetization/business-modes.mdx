Business mode decides how a linked device delivers what the customer paid for. Each linked device uses one mode: **Service** or **Vending**.

Business mode is different from prepaid and postpaid. Prepaid and postpaid describe _when_ the customer pays. Business mode describes _what happens_ after the order is confirmed.

## Service or Vending

|  | Service | Vending |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What the customer buys | Time-bound or usage-bound use of the device | One or more physical items |
| What happens after payment | A session starts on the device | The device dispenses the paid units |
| Items per order | One item per order | Multiple items in one cart |
| Payment type | Prepaid or postpaid | Prepaid only |
| Per-device inventory | Not used | Vending Stock per device |
| Session tracking | Yes, with start, end, and duration | No, the order completes at dispense |
| Kiosk mode | Not available | Available on supported devices |
| Examples | Laundry, massage chair, EV charging, locker storage | Snack machine, drink dispenser, capsule vendor |

## Set the business mode

1. Open **Monetization** in the dashboard and select your merchant.
2. Go to **Linked Devices**.
3. Open the setup drawer for the device you want to configure.
4. Find the **Business Mode** card.
5. Choose **Service** or **Vending**.

The dashboard confirms the change after it is saved.

\[image:linked device setup drawer showing the Business Mode card with Service and Vending options\]

<Note>
  Business mode is set per linked device, not per merchant. One merchant can have a service device and a vending device at the same time.
</Note>

## What changes when you switch

Switching business mode changes the dashboard setup and the customer experience on the public page.

| Area | Service | Vending |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Device drawer menu | Overview, Sessions, Map Entity, Flow | Adds **Vending Stock** |
| Public page | Single-item checkout | Cart with quantity per item |
| Catalog items used | Prepaid and postpaid items | Prepaid items only |
| After payment | Session controls on the order page | Dispense and receipt |

Switching a device to **Service** also turns **Kiosk mode** off, because kiosk is a vending-only option.

## Choose the right mode

Choose **Service** when the customer pays to use the device for a period of time or for measured usage, and the order should stay open until the session ends.

Choose **Vending** when the customer pays for physical units and the order is complete as soon as the device dispenses them.

<Warning>
  Review your catalog after switching modes. Postpaid and usage-based items cannot be sold from a vending device, so they stay unavailable on that device's public page.
</Warning>

## Checklist

Before opening the device to customers:

- The device uses the intended business mode.
- Catalog items match the mode's supported payment types.
- Vending devices have stock reviewed for every item sold on that device.
- Flow settings, verification, and stop behavior are tested in the selected mode.
- The public page has been reviewed end to end.

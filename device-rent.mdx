firmngin charges a small monthly rent for every device beyond your free allowance. The rent is taken from your **usage credit wallet**, not from a card. In your first month you only pay for the days left in that month.

<Note>
  This page is about what **you** pay firmngin to run your devices. It is not the **Device Billing** feature, which lets you charge your own customers. For that, see [Hardware Monetization](/monetization).
</Note>

## Pricing

The current price per device per month is always listed on the [firmngin Pricing page](https://firmngin.dev/pricing), and your dashboard shows it too.

- **The price can change.** A new price applies from the next charge: either a new device or the next 1st. Charges already made are **never** re-charged, refunded, or adjusted.
- **$0.50 is only an example.** The examples on this page use an example price of $0.50 per device per month so the math is easy to follow. Your actual price is the one on the [Pricing page](https://firmngin.dev/pricing).

## Key rules at a glance

| Rule | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| Free devices | Your first **5 devices** are free. The free ones are always your oldest devices. |
| Renewal date | Rent renews on the **1st of every month at 00:00 UTC** (07:00 WIB). It is the same date for every account. |
| First month | A new paid device only costs the days left in the current month, including the day you create it. |
| How you pay | Rent comes out of your usage credit wallet. Your card is never charged automatically. |
| Creating a device | You can create a paid device as long as your balance covers its first-month cost. |
| No double charges | A device is never charged twice in the same month. |
| Disabled devices | **Still charged.** Only deleting or archiving a device stops the rent, from the next month. |
| Price changes | A new price applies from the next charge. Past charges never change. |

## Your first month is prorated

When you create a device beyond your free allowance, you only pay for the rest of the current month:

> **First-month cost = monthly price × days left in the month ÷ days in the month**

Days left includes the day you create the device, and dates follow UTC. From the next 1st onward, the device costs the full monthly price.

| Created on (UTC) | Days left | You pay now | Next charge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Oct 1, 2026 | 31 of 31 | **$0.50** | Nov 1, $0.50 |
| Oct 28, 2026 | 4 of 31 | **$0.0646** | Nov 1, $0.50 |
| Nov 10, 2026 | 21 of 30 | **$0.35** | Dec 1, $0.50 |

## When your balance is too low

If your wallet cannot cover the monthly rent on the 1st:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Grace period">
    Your account gets a **7-day grace period**. Your devices keep working as normal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read-only">
    If you have not topped up when grace ends, your devices above the free allowance become **read-only**: they cannot connect or be controlled. Nothing is deleted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Top up to restore">
    Top up your wallet at any time. Once the unpaid rent is paid, everything works again automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Emails

We email you when your balance is too low for the upcoming monthly charge, when a grace period starts, shortly before it ends, and if your devices become read-only. Every email includes a top-up link.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will I be charged again every month?">
    Yes. Every device beyond your free allowance is charged the full monthly price on the 1st of each month (UTC), until you delete or archive it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I get a refund if I delete a device mid-month?">
    No. Rent is paid in advance for the month and is not refunded. The device stops costing anything from the next month.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My devices are read-only. How do I get them back?">
    Top up your wallet. Once the unpaid rent is paid, your devices come back automatically. You do not need to contact support.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

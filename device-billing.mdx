title: "Device billing & proration"
description: "How firmngin charges for devices beyond your free allowance: first-month proration, monthly renewal on the 1st, grace period, and billing emails"
---

firmngin charges a monthly rent for every device beyond your free allowance. The rent is taken from your **usage credit wallet**, not from a card. Your first month is prorated, so you only pay for the days left in the month when you add a device.

<Info>
  Device prices can change. The current price per device per month is always shown on the [Pricing page](https://firmngin.dev/pricing) and in the dashboard. The simulations on this page use an **example price of $0.50/device/month**.
</Info>

## Key rules at a glance

| Rule | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Free devices | Your first devices are free. The default allowance is **5 devices**. The free devices are always your oldest ones, by creation order. |
| Billing date | Every billable device renews on the **1st of each month at 00:00 UTC** (07:00 WIB). The date is the same for every account. |
| First month | A new billable device is charged a **prorated** amount for the days left in the current month, including the day you create it. |
| Payment method | Rent is deducted from your usage credit wallet. There are no automatic card charges. |
| Balance check | You can create a billable device when your wallet balance covers the prorated amount. |
| Double charges | Each device is charged at most once per month. |
| Price changes | A new price applies from the next charge. Past charges are never adjusted. |

## Free devices

Each account gets a free device allowance. By default, your first **5 devices** are free. The dashboard shows the allowance that applies to your account.

- The free slots always go to your **oldest devices**, ordered by creation time.
- Only devices beyond the allowance are billed. If you have 7 devices and 5 are free, you pay for 2.
- Devices in deleted or archived workspaces do not count toward the total.

## Monthly billing on the 1st

All billable devices renew on the **1st of every month at 00:00 UTC**. This is the same moment for every account, wherever you are:

| Your time zone | Local time of the monthly charge |
| --- | --- |
| UTC | 1st, 00:00 |
| WIB (UTC+7) | 1st, 07:00 |
| PDT (UTC-7) | Last day of the previous month, 17:00 |

Each device is charged the full monthly price, exactly as configured. The billing job runs every 6 hours, so the deduction appears in your wallet history within about 6 hours after 00:00 UTC on the 1st.

## First-month proration

When you create a device that is beyond your free allowance, firmngin charges only for the rest of the current month:

```text
remaining_days = days_in_month - creation_day + 1
prorated_amount = price × remaining_days / days_in_month
```

- All dates use **UTC**. A device created on Oct 28 at 06:00 WIB is created on Oct 27 UTC.
- `remaining_days` **includes the creation day**. Creating on Oct 28 in a 31-day month gives 4 days (28, 29, 30, 31).
- The amount is kept to at most **4 decimal places** and is always **rounded up** at the 4th decimal. For example, $0.064516… becomes $0.0646. An amount that already fits in 4 decimals is not changed: $0.125 stays $0.125.
- The price used is the price configured at the moment of the charge. Each wallet entry records the unit price, the days charged, and the amount.
- From the next 1st onward, the device is charged the full monthly price.

<Note>
  Because amounts are rounded up at the 4th decimal, you never pay more than $0.0001 above the exact prorated value. For example, 1 day of a $0.50 device in a 31-day month is $0.016129…, charged as $0.0162.
</Note>

## Balance required to create a device

When a new device lands beyond your free allowance, firmngin checks your wallet before the device is created:

- If your balance is **at least the prorated amount**, the device is created and the prorated rent is deducted right away.
- If your balance is lower, the request is refused with HTTP `402` and no device is created. Top up your wallet and try again.

Devices inside your free allowance never need a balance.

### Preview the cost

Before you create a device, you can preview the cost: how much you pay now, how many days that covers, and when and how much the next charge is. For example: "$0.0646 now (4 days), next charge Nov 1, $0.50".

The preview is available from the API:

```http
GET /api/v1/users/usage/device-rent-quote
```

```json
{
  "data": {
    "charge_now": "0.0646",
    "remaining_days": 4,
    "days_in_month": 31,
    "full_price": "0.5",
    "next_charge_at": "2026-11-01T00:00:00Z",
    "next_charge_amount": "0.5",
    "prorated": true,
    "is_free_slot": false,
    "can_afford": true,
    "billable_devices": 0,
    "free_devices": 5,
    "mode": "credit"
  }
}
```

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `charge_now` | Amount deducted if you create a device now. `0` when the device lands in a free slot. |
| `remaining_days` / `days_in_month` | The days the first charge covers, counting today (UTC). |
| `full_price` | The current monthly price per device. |
| `next_charge_at` / `next_charge_amount` | The next renewal: the 1st of next month at 00:00 UTC, at the full price. |
| `is_free_slot` | `true` when the new device would be inside your free allowance. |
| `can_afford` | `false` when your balance is lower than `charge_now`. Creating the device would return `402`. |

### Billing details in the create response

The response to creating a device (`POST /api/v1/devices`) includes a `billing` object with the same fields. Its `charge_now` value is read from your wallet history, so it always equals the amount actually deducted.

```json
"billing": {
  "charge_now": "0.0646",
  "prorated": true,
  "remaining_days": 4,
  "days_in_month": 31,
  "full_price": "0.5",
  "next_charge_at": "2026-11-01T00:00:00Z",
  "next_charge_amount": "0.5",
  "is_free_slot": false
}
```

## No double charges

Each charge is recorded per device, per month. Once a device is paid for a month, it is not charged again that month, even if the billing job runs many times.

If the charge at creation was missed for any reason, the billing job charges the device later in the same month. It still uses the prorated amount from the device's creation date, never the full month.

## What stops and what keeps billing

| Device state | Billing |
| --- | --- |
| Active | Billed every month while it is beyond the free allowance. |
| Disabled | **Still billed.** Disabling a device does not free its slot. |
| Deleted | Stops billing from the next month. **No refund** for the current month. |
| Archived, or in an archived workspace | Stops billing from the next month. **No refund** for the current month. |

<Warning>
  Deleting a device and creating a new one in the same month charges the new device separately. Rent is tied to each device, not to a reusable slot.
</Warning>

## When your balance is too low

If your wallet cannot cover the monthly rent:

<Steps>
  <Step title="The charge fails and grace starts">
    The rent for that device is not deducted. Your account enters a **7-day grace period**. Your devices keep working during grace, and you get the grace notice email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You top up">
    A top-up ends the grace period. As soon as the top-up is confirmed, firmngin charges the unpaid rent from your new balance. If that fails for any reason, the billing job retries on its next run, within about 6 hours. You do not need to do anything else.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grace ends without a top-up">
    Workspaces and devices above your free allowance become **read-only**. Read-only devices cannot be used at all: their authentication and authorization are rejected, so they cannot connect, publish or subscribe, call the device HTTP API (it returns `402 Payment Required`), or receive commands from the dashboard or the API. A device that is already connected is refused on its next publish or subscribe and cannot reconnect. You also cannot edit them or add new devices. Nothing is deleted. You get the read-only notice email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restore access">
    Top up your wallet. Once the top-up is confirmed and the unpaid rent is charged, your devices and workspaces switch back on automatically, and their authentication and authorization work again right away. If the charge cannot run immediately, the billing job retries within about 6 hours. Devices that an administrator disabled for other reasons stay disabled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Billing emails

firmngin sends billing emails to the account owner only when real charges are active. Each email is sent at most once for the period or grace episode it describes.

| Email | When it is sent | What it contains |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upcoming charge reminder | About 3 days before the 1st (from the 3rd-to-last day of the month, UTC), only when your balance is lower than next month's device rent. | Charge date (1st, 00:00 UTC), number of billable devices, amount due, current balance, shortfall, and a top-up link. |
| Grace period notice | Once, when a monthly charge fails because the balance is too low. | Amount due, current balance, shortfall, number of affected devices, grace end date (UTC and local), what happens after grace, and a top-up link. |
| Grace reminder | Once, about 48 hours before grace ends, if the rent is still unpaid. | The same details as the grace notice, with the time left. |
| Read-only notice | Once, after grace ends and resources have actually been switched to read-only. | Amount due, balance, shortfall, what is restricted, and how to restore access: top up, and once the rent is paid, access is restored automatically. |

Every email includes a top-up link, and times are shown in UTC and WIB. If you top up before an email is due, that email is not sent. If an email fails to send, it is retried on the next billing run, and the charge itself is never affected.

## Price changes

The device price can change over time. Check the [Pricing page](https://firmngin.dev/pricing) for the current price.

- A new price applies from the **next** charge, either a new device or the next 1st.
- Charges already made are **never** re-billed, refunded, or adjusted.
- Each wallet entry records the unit price, days, and amount used, so your history stays accurate after a price change.

## Simulations

All simulations use an **example price of $0.50/device/month** and the default allowance of 5 free devices. Your actual price may differ; see [Pricing](https://firmngin.dev/pricing).

### Prorated first charge

| Created on (UTC) | Days in month | Remaining days | Exact amount | Charged now | Next charge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Oct 1, 2026 | 31 | 31 | $0.50 | **$0.50** | Nov 1, $0.50 |
| Oct 28, 2026 | 31 | 4 | $0.064516… | **$0.0646** | Nov 1, $0.50 |
| Oct 31, 2026 | 31 | 1 | $0.016129… | **$0.0162** | Nov 1, $0.50 |
| Nov 10, 2026 | 30 | 21 | $0.35 | **$0.35** | Dec 1, $0.50 |
| Feb 15, 2027 (not a leap year) | 28 | 14 | $0.25 | **$0.25** | Mar 1, $0.50 |
| Feb 15, 2028 (leap year) | 29 | 15 | $0.258620… | **$0.2587** | Mar 1, $0.50 |
| Feb 22, 2027 | 28 | 7 | $0.125 | **$0.125** (already 4 decimals, not rounded) | Mar 1, $0.50 |

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Create a device on Oct 28">
    1. October has 31 days. Remaining days: 31 − 28 + 1 = **4**.
    2. Amount: $0.50 × 4 / 31 = $0.064516…, rounded up to **$0.0646**.
    3. You need at least $0.0646 in your wallet. With $2.00, the device is created and your balance becomes $1.9354.
    4. On Nov 1 at 00:00 UTC (07:00 WIB), the device is charged the full **$0.50**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Create a device on Oct 1">
    Remaining days: 31 − 1 + 1 = 31, so the device pays the full **$0.50** for October. The next charge is $0.50 on Nov 1.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Create a device on Feb 15 (leap and non-leap years)">
    - 2027 (28 days): 28 − 15 + 1 = 14 days. $0.50 × 14 / 28 = **$0.25**.
    - 2028 (29 days): 29 − 15 + 1 = 15 days. $0.50 × 15 / 29 = $0.258620…, rounded up to **$0.2587**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### An account with 7 devices and 5 free

You already have 5 devices, all free. You add device 6 on **Oct 28** and device 7 on **Nov 10**.

| Month | Device 6 | Device 7 | Total for the month |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| October | $0.0646 (Oct 28, 4/31 days) | — | **$0.0646** |
| November | $0.50 (Nov 1) | $0.35 (Nov 10, 21/30 days) | **$0.85** |
| December | $0.50 (Dec 1) | $0.50 (Dec 1) | **$1.00** |
| Every month after | $0.50 | $0.50 | **$1.00** |

If you delete device 2 in December, device 6 moves into the free allowance from the next charge, because the free slots always go to your oldest devices. From January you pay for 1 device: **$0.50**.

### Low balance, grace, and top-up

Your wallet holds **$0.10**. You have 5 free devices and create device 6 on **Oct 28**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Oct 28: device created">
    The prorated amount is $0.0646. Your balance of $0.10 covers it, so the device is created. Balance: **$0.0354**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Oct 29: upcoming charge reminder">
    October has 31 days, so reminders start on Oct 29 (UTC). Next month's rent is 1 × $0.50 = $0.50. Your balance of $0.0354 is short by **$0.4646**, so you get one reminder email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Nov 1, 00:00 UTC (07:00 WIB): charge fails">
    The $0.50 charge cannot be paid. Grace starts and you get the grace notice: amount due $0.50, balance $0.0354, shortfall $0.4646, 1 device affected, grace ends around Nov 8, 00:00 UTC (07:00 WIB).
  </Step>
  <Step title="About Nov 6: grace reminder">
    About 48 hours before grace ends, you get one reminder if the rent is still unpaid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Top up">
    You top up $5.00 on Nov 7. Grace ends, and the $0.50 is deducted right after the top-up is confirmed. Balance: **$4.5354**. No read-only notice is sent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="If you do not top up">
    Around Nov 8, device 6 becomes read-only and you get the read-only notice. When you top up later, for example $5.00 on Nov 10, the $0.50 is deducted right after the top-up is confirmed (balance **$4.5354**) and device 6 switches back on automatically. From then on the device can authenticate and send or receive data again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Time zones

The charge happens at the same instant for everyone: the 1st at 00:00 UTC.

| Location | When the Nov 1 charge happens locally |
| --- | --- |
| Jakarta (WIB, UTC+7) | Nov 1, 07:00 |
| London (GMT, UTC+0) | Nov 1, 00:00 |
| San Francisco (PDT, UTC-7) | **Oct 31, 17:00** |

Proration also uses the UTC date. If you are in San Francisco and create a device on Oct 31 at 18:00 PDT, it is already Nov 1 in UTC. It is billed as a November device: $0.50 × 30 / 30 = **$0.50**, and the next charge is Dec 1.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will I be charged again every month?">
    Yes. Every device beyond your free allowance is charged the full monthly price on the 1st of each month (UTC), until you delete or archive it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my card charged automatically?">
    No. Rent is deducted from your usage credit wallet. Top up the wallet to keep enough balance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I get a refund if I delete a device mid-month?">
    No. Rent is prepaid for the month and is not refunded. The device stops billing from the next month.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are disabled devices billed?">
    Yes. Only deleted or archived devices stop billing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I be charged twice for the same device in one month?">
    No. Each device is charged at most once per month.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if the price changes?">
    The new price applies from the next charge. Past charges stay as they are.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why was I charged $0.0162 for one day?">
    Prorated amounts keep at most 4 decimal places and are always rounded up at the 4th decimal. One day of a $0.50 device in a 31-day month is $0.016129…, which rounds up to $0.0162.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My devices are read-only. How do I get them back?">
    Top up your wallet. Once the top-up is confirmed and the unpaid rent is paid, your devices switch back on automatically and can authenticate, connect, and send or receive data again. While read-only, they cannot authenticate or be authorized for anything. You do not need to contact support.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did I get an email before the 1st?">
    From 3 days before the 1st, firmngin checks whether your balance covers next month's rent. If it does not, you get one reminder with the shortfall and a top-up link. If your balance is enough, no email is sent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which devices are free?">
    Your oldest devices, by creation time, up to your free allowance (5 by default).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

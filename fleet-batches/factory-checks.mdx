---
title: "Factory setup and unit checks"
description: "Prepare each physical board and check that the finished unit works before offering it for sale."
---

A registered device record and a finished product are separate steps. Use factory setup to prepare each physical board, then check the completed unit before packing it.

## Prepare the production station

Have the correct boards, reliable power, a suitable USB connection, and the tested firmware for the product available. Select the workspace and batch that correspond to the units on the workbench.

The **firmngin-factory** application runs in a terminal on your production computer. It supports factory registration, firmware flashing, and setting up each unit's own device identity.

1. Get the application from the [public Factory CLI distribution](https://github.com/firmngin/factory-cli) and follow its installation instructions.
2. Open a terminal and enter `firmngin-factory` to launch the interactive application.
3. Choose **Sign in (Factory)** and sign in with the Firmngin account used for production. Review the requested factory access.
4. At **Select Workspace**, choose the workspace you prepared in the dashboard.
5. Have the tested factory firmware and the unit's network setup available.

Use the application version and firmware appropriate to your production hardware. The firmware needs current Firmngin factory provisioning support.

## Prepare each board

Connect one board, then follow these steps in the interactive application:

1. Choose **Provision Unit**.
2. At **Select Batch**, choose the batch you created. The application selects the next available unit within that batch.
3. At **Select Firmware**, choose **Cloud library** to use your uploaded master firmware, or **Local .merged.bin file** to use a file on your computer. Select the tested image for this product.
4. At **Flash & Provision**, select the board's serial port. Keep **Skip flash** unchecked for a fresh board, then press Enter to start.
5. Let the application install the image, set up the unit's identity, and wait for the board to restart and reconnect. Wait for **Congratulations!** and the success result.
6. Match the finished board to its device record and keep it identified. Connect the next board, then choose **Provision next device** when you are ready to repeat the process.

Each physical unit needs its own device identity. Keep completed and unfinished boards separate so that a board is not confused with another unit's record.

For fresh-board master firmware, use the complete **`.merged.bin`** image uploaded as **As Master Firmware Fleet**. See [Master firmware for fresh boards](/firmware-master-fleet) for preparing the correct file.

The factory workflow checks the new online connection before completing setup. Writing firmware alone does not complete provisioning. If a step fails, keep that board aside and resolve the reported issue before treating it as complete.

After setup, return to **Fleet Batches** and review the unit's factory status. A successful factory setup is recorded as **provisioned**.

<Note>
  Provisioned means factory setup was recorded as complete. Check the unit's actual operation before treating it as ready to sell.
</Note>

## Check the finished unit

Use the same checks for every unit of the product. Adapt the expected results to what your device is designed to do.

| Check | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Unit identity | The physical unit matches the device record and the label you will attach. |
| First connection | The powered device connects and appears online in Firmngin. |
| Sensor readings | The expected readings appear and are plausible for the test conditions. |
| Controls | Each supported control produces the expected physical result. |
| Restart | After restarting, the unit reconnects and still behaves correctly. |
| Customer setup | The supplied instructions work for the setup customers will perform. |
| Physical condition | The power connection, enclosure, connectors, and accessories are ready for delivery. |

Open the device from the expanded batch to review its readings and controls. If a unit fails a check, keep it separate from passing units while you investigate.

## Prepare the handover

Keep the unit and its identifying label together throughout testing and packing. Include the setup guide, required accessories, and support details promised with your product.

Keep a record of which unit was delivered against each customer order. If a code is replaced before shipment, update its printed label too.

When a unit passes your checks, it can move to your stock and sales process. Payment, delivery, customer access, and warranty arrangements remain part of the product experience you manage.

Continue to [Sell and deliver devices](/fleet-batches/sell-and-deliver) for stock, unit labels, and sale records. Read [Customer access](/fleet-batches/customer-access) to prepare the buyer experience.

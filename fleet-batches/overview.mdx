title: "Fleet Batches"
description: "Prepare a repeatable device product, produce a batch, and manage units for sale."
---

Fleet Batches helps developers prepare and track many devices made from the same device template. Use it to organize a production run, follow each unit through factory setup, and keep track of stock and recorded sales.

For example, you might develop a temperature monitor, prove it works on one board, and then prepare a batch of 50 units for customers.

## From prototype to product

| Stage | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| Prepare the product | Choose the hardware, test the firmware, and define a reusable device template. |
| Create the batch | Register the required number of devices with a shared base name. |
| Prepare each board | Use the factory application to install the firmware and set up each unit's own device identity. |
| Check the units | Confirm connection, readings, controls, and behavior after restarting. |
| Prepare for sale | Organize ready units, their labels, and the information customers need. |
| Record and support sales | Keep the buyer and order reference with the unit, then manage returns or changes when needed. |

Creating a batch registers devices in Firmngin. Physical boards still need factory setup and testing before they are ready to ship.

## How selling works

You manage the customer sale through your own store, sales team, or other sales channel. Prepare the price, payment method, delivery, warranty, and support arrangements for your product.

Fleet Batches helps you track the units and record sales. A recorded sale is a unit record; it does not collect payment or arrange shipping.

There is no built-in buyer application in this workflow. If your product needs an application for buyers to sign in, add a device, or use its features, prepare that experience as part of your product. A QR label or claim code alone does not provide it.

## Where to find it

Open **Devices**, then **Fleet Batches** under **Factories** in the dashboard.

The batch list shows the number of registered devices and **Provisioning remaining**. Expand a batch to see its units and their factory setup status.
